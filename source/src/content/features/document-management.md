## The document types you can keep

You decide what belongs in a property's records. Document storage works for whatever you need to keep with a property, including leases and rental agreements, receipts for repairs and supplies, inspection notes, insurance policies, and other records you want to find again. When you upload a document you choose a document type, and the app saves the document together with its name, type, size, and upload date.

## A retrieval workflow built around the property

Finding a document should not require remembering which folder or email thread it lives in. In ManageState, documents live on the property screen. A concrete example: after the spring inspection you photograph the signed inspection report. You open the property, upload the photo, and set the document type to inspection. Six months later, when the tenant asks about the paint condition noted at inspection, you open the property, scroll to its documents, and the report is right there with the date it was added.

## Documents next to the numbers

Keeping documents beside the financial records they relate to saves time at year end. The receipt for that water heater repair and the repair transaction live on the same property screen, so you can look at both without jumping between apps. When you are ready to hand records to an accountant, the [tax export page](/features/tax-export/) explains how property records and documents fit together.

## Your documents and your privacy

Document storage is part of your property records in your ManageState account. For details on what data the app collects and how analytics work, read the [privacy policy](/privacy/).
