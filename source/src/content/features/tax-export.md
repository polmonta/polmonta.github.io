## What your records include

Everything you record during the year becomes part of your export. Properties, income and expense transactions with their categories, recurring items, and monthly summaries are all kept per property, and documents stay attached to the property they belong to, as explained on the [document management page](/features/document-management/). The more consistently you record during the year, the more complete the workbook is at tax time.

## Export formats you can rely on

ManageState exports your data as an XLSX workbook, the spreadsheet format most accountants can open. The workbook contains sheets for properties, transactions, recurring items, and monthly summaries, built from the records you entered. From your iPhone you can share the file with anyone you choose, including your accountant.

## Handing your records to an accountant

A concrete workflow. All year you record rent and expenses as they happen. In February, you open the export screen, choose the property or properties you need, and generate the workbook. You share the file with your accountant through the usual share sheet, then meet to go over the same numbers you have been reviewing all year. Your accountant can review the records you kept, and ManageState does not prepare your return or tell you what to claim.

## ManageState does not provide tax advice

ManageState is a records tool. It does not provide tax, legal, or accounting advice, it does not determine what you can claim, and it does not prepare or file tax returns. Rules vary by jurisdiction and by your situation, so talk to a qualified tax professional about your records and your return.
