## What the template includes

The CSV uses one row per transaction with these columns:

- **property:** the rental property the transaction belongs to.
- **date:** the transaction date in YYYY-MM-DD format.
- **type:** income or expense.
- **category:** your own label, such as rent, maintenance, utilities, or insurance.
- **description:** a short note so you can recognize the transaction later.
- **amount:** the amount in decimal form, for example 125.00.
- **currency:** the three-letter currency code, for example USD or EUR.
- **document_reference:** the receipt or document reference, when you have one.

[Download the rental income and expense template (CSV)](/downloads/rental-income-expense-template.csv)

## How to use the template

Open the CSV in the spreadsheet app you already use, delete the example row, and fill one row per transaction as it happens. Keep the header row unchanged, keep dates in YYYY-MM-DD format, and enter amounts in decimal form so totals add up cleanly. The file includes the same guidance in its first lines, so the instructions travel with the template.

## Categories are organization, not tax advice

Categories such as rent, maintenance, utilities, and insurance are organizational labels that keep your records readable. They are not a statement about what you can deduct. What counts as a deductible expense depends on the rules for your situation and your jurisdiction, so treat the categories as filing structure and let your accountant or tax adviser decide what to claim. The [tax record guide](/guides/landlord-tax-record-app/) explains how organized records support that conversation.

## Where the filled-in template goes from here

A filled-in template becomes the working record you review each month. Check income, expenses, and net income per property, and catch double entries while they are still easy to fix. If you would rather keep those records on iPhone with each receipt attached to its property, the [financial tracking page](/features/financial-control/) and the [landlord expense tracker guide](/guides/landlord-expense-tracker/) show how ManageState organizes the same fields for you.
